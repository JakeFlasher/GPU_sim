# Plan scope

Plans are short technical context, not workflow policy. Use them to choose the
next simulator seam or capture command, then confirm behavior with the live
path and current outputs. Root `AGENTS.md` controls all work in this directory.

Superseded plans are not implementation queues. Do not restore their former
authority, receipt, audit, provenance, hash, validation, approval, freeze, or
review machinery; the permanent alpha ban in the root `AGENTS.md` applies even
when an old plan still contains one of those words.
